Apps in progress

One app in active development right now.

Raya's Book Shelf

In development

Kids fill notebooks with drawings and stories that usually end up in a recycling bin. Raya's Book Shelf turns those paper pages into a shared, ongoing book: a parent photographs each page as it happens, and family and friends actually get to see the work. It's not just a share, it's a running archive of how a kid's creativity has grown, page by page.
