About

A small LLC, doing two things on purpose.

Bridges to Sunshine LLC was formed in 2025 to build mobile apps, and to build websites for small businesses. Both come from the same instinct: most of the tools a small team or a family actually needs don't require a big platform. They need something built for exactly what they're trying to do.

The work stays close to the ground. Local business sites that don't need a marketing department to update. Apps built for the people already doing the thing, not for a growth chart.

Right now that means Raya's Book Shelf: an app for the pile of drawings and stories a kid makes on paper, most of which usually never makes it past the fridge door. It's the first product to come out of the company, and it's still in active development.

Community is the filter for what we take on. If a project doesn't make a neighborhood, a family, or a small business better off, it's not a fit.
