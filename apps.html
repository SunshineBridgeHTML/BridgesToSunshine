Raya's Book Shelf

In development

Kids fill notebooks, loose pages, and sketchbooks with writing and drawings that usually get tossed once the fridge runs out of room. Raya's Book Shelf gives that work somewhere to live.

A parent or guardian photographs each page as their kid finishes it. The app turns those photos into an ongoing, page-ordered book — readable like a real book, not a photo dump. Family and friends who'd otherwise never see the work can open a shared link and actually read it, react to it, watch it build over time.

It's not only a way to share one drawing. It's a running archive of how a kid's creative work has grown, page by page, book by book — the kind of thing that's usually gone within a week of being made.
